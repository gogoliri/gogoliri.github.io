About

I am a research intern at the Trustworthy and Adversarial Computing Lab at Aalto University, working on adversarial examples for cyber-physical vision systems: attacks realized in the physical world — printed patches, modified objects, AR/VR — rather than purely digital perturbations. The work is gradient-based optimization of attacks on deep computer vision models under physical constraints.

Before that I spent a year with the Visual AI System Research team at Nokia Technologies and Aalto University, on super-resolution post-filters for Versatile Video Coding, low-rank adaptation for finetuning, and quantization-aware training. My MSc thesis, written at the Signal Analysis and Machine Intelligence group in Tampere, recast joint compression–segmentation training as constrained optimization over the rate–quality–performance trade-off.

Parts of this work have gone into standardization: two contributions to MPEG and JVET, one of which was adopted into the Common Test Conditions used to develop the Video Coding for Machines standard.
